# health.gov.au — 住宿型养老提供者责任与记录保存（网页摘要）

## 1. Responsibilities of residential aged care providers
- **URL**: https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/residential-aged-care/responsibilities-of-providers
- **页面标注**: "Date last updated: 23 December 2025"
- **Publisher**: Department of Health, Disability and Ageing
- **版权**: © Commonwealth；个人/机构内部使用、不得商业使用（https://www.health.gov.au/using-our-websites/copyright）

要点（原文摘录/转述）：
- Residential aged care provider（registration category 6）须：符合 Aged Care Quality Standards；正确使用并报告资金；**have an incident management system in place**；24/7 至少一名 RN 在岗；每季度达到 direct care minutes；公布住宿价格等。
- 对每位住户：创建 Aged Care Entry Record (ACER)；讨论费用；签署 service agreement / accommodation agreement 等；进行 ongoing care discussions。
- Reporting：Aged Care Financial Report（含 audited Care Minutes Performance Statement）、Quarterly Financial Report（含 care time reporting）、**quality indicator data**、每月 24/7 RN coverage、向 ACQSC 通报 SIRS reportable incidents 等。
- 感染防控：须按 Quality Standards Outcomes 4.2 与 5.2 管理呼吸道传染病，提供并**记录**住户与员工的流感/COVID-19 疫苗接种。

## 2. Guide to Aged Care Law — 记录保存
- Chapter 3（适用于所有提供者的义务）: https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/guide-to-aged-care-law/chapter-3-registered-providers-aged-care-workers-and-aged-care-digital-platform-operators/obligations-that-apply-to-all-providers
  - 原文："Providers must keep records about: the services they delivered and the people they delivered them to; complaints and feedback, including how they manage and resolve these; responsible persons and aged care workers, and any claims of misconduct; reports to the department and how they meet their reporting requirements."
  - 须让老人访问其个人信息，并按其意愿协助 registered supporters 访问。
- Chapter 7 Keeping records: https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/guide-to-aged-care-law/chapter-7-information-management/keeping-records （Date last updated: 28 October 2025）
  - Former providers 须在注销后保存相关记录 **7 年**。
  - 现行提供者的具体保存期限 ⚠️ 需核实（需查 Aged Care Rules 2025 原文）。

## 对 careKind 的意义
- careKind 生成的每条草稿与最终护士审核版都属于"services they delivered"记录的一部分 → 需要可追溯（谁口述、谁审核、何时修改）、可导出、长期保存。
- 住户与 supporter 有权访问个人信息 → 系统需支持按住户导出记录。
