# Incident Triage

1. Read `03-legal-compliance/data-breach-response-plan.md`, `03-legal-compliance/ai-governance.md` §6 and `09-templates/incident-report.md`.
2. Create `03-legal-compliance/incidents/INC-YYYYMMDD-NN.md` from the template. Record only facts, with timestamps (Australia/Melbourne).
3. Give the immediate CONTAIN checklist relevant to the incident type.
4. Calculate and state the NDB assessment deadline (discovery + 30 days). List who may need notifying (OAIC, individuals, provider per the DPA, insurer, ACSC), but do not send anything. 🧑 Notification decisions belong to the Privacy Officer and legal counsel.
5. Do not paste any sensitive data into the report. Refer to the systems or records where the data lives.
6. Remind the user to contact the cyber insurer early (the policy may require it).
