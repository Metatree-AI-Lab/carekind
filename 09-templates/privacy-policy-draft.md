## 1. About this policy
{{careKind AI Pty Ltd}} (ABN {{}}) ("careKind", "we", "us") is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and applicable state and territory health records laws.

## 2. Who we are and what we do
careKind provides an AI-assisted documentation tool to aged care providers. Care staff use careKind to draft care records from their own spoken or written notes. Every draft is reviewed and approved by a staff member before it is saved. careKind does not diagnose, recommend treatment or make clinical decisions.

## 3. Personal information we handle
- **Information about people receiving care** (including health information, which is sensitive information), provided by care staff on behalf of our aged care provider customers.
- **Voice recordings** of care staff dictating notes.
- **Information about our customers' staff** (name, work email, role, professional registration number, usage logs).
- **Business contacts and website visitors** (name, contact details, enquiries, cookies and analytics).

## 4. How we collect it
Primarily from our aged care provider customers and their staff through the careKind app. Providers are responsible for informing people receiving care (and their representatives) about the use of careKind and obtaining any consents required.

## 5. How we use it
- To provide the service: transcribe dictation, generate draft records, and deliver approved records to the provider's systems.
- To maintain security, provide support, and meet legal obligations.
- **We do not use information about people receiving care to train general-purpose AI models**, and we do not sell personal information.
