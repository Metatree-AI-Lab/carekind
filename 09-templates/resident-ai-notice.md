# Template: Notice to residents and representatives about AI-assisted documentation

> For aged care providers to adapt. ⚠️ Provider to review with its own privacy/legal advisers. Offer translated and easy-read versions.

**How we use careKind AI to help write care records**

Our care staff sometimes use a tool called careKind AI to help write notes about your care. Here's what that means:

- **Staff stay responsible.** A staff member speaks or types their notes, careKind drafts the record, and the staff member checks, corrects and approves it before it is saved.
- **It doesn't make decisions about your care.** careKind only helps with writing. Decisions about your care are always made by your care team.
- **Your information stays in Australia.** It is stored securely, isn't sold, and isn't used to train general AI products.
- **Voice recordings are deleted** shortly after the note is approved.
- **You have choices.** If you'd prefer that AI tools aren't used for your records, or you have questions, talk to {{contact role}} at {{facility}}. Choosing this won't affect your care.
