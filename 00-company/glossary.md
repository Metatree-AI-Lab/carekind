# 术语表 (Glossary)

Owner: Lightman (PO) · Last reviewed: 04/10/2026

## Aged care 行业
| 术语 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| Aged Care Act 2024 | 新的联邦 aged care 法，2025-11-01 生效，以权利为本（rights-based），取代 Aged Care Act 1997 |
| ACQSC | Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission，aged care 监管机构 |
| Strengthened Quality Standards | 新版 7 项质量标准：1 The Individual · 2 The Organisation · 3 The Care and Services · 4 The Environment · 5 Clinical Care · 6 Food and Nutrition · 7 The Residential Community |
| Statement of Rights | 新法下老人的权利声明，provider 必须遵守 |
| SIRS | Serious Incident Response Scheme，严重事件报告制度（Priority 1 / Priority 2 时限不同） |
| AN-ACC | Australian National Aged Care Classification，residential care 的资助分类模型 |
| Care minutes | 强制照护分钟数目标（每住户每天平均 215 分钟，其中 RN 44 分钟） |
| Residential aged care home | 养老院（旧称 RACF） |
| Support at Home | 2025-11-01 起取代 Home Care Packages 的居家照护项目 |
| Provider | 获批的 aged care 服务提供方（我们的客户） |
| Older person / Resident | 接受照护的老人（新法用语倾向 "older person"） |
| Restrictive practices | 限制性措施（化学/身体/环境等），有严格记录要求 |

## 护理角色
| 术语 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| RN | Registered Nurse，注册护士（AHPRA 注册） |
| EN | Enrolled Nurse，登记护士（AHPRA 注册，在 RN 监督下工作） |
| PCW / AIN | Personal Care Worker / Assistant in Nursing，护理员（非 AHPRA 注册） |
| DON / Clinical Care Manager | Director of Nursing / 护理经理 |
| NMBA | Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia |
| AHPRA | Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency |

## 文书类型
Progress notes · Incident report · Handover · Care plan / Care and services plan · Wound chart · Behaviour chart · BGL / vital signs chart · Falls risk assessment · Family/representative communication log · Medication chart (我们不碰用药决策)

## 法律/监管
| 术语 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| APPs | Australian Privacy Principles（13 条），Privacy Act 1988 |
| OAIC | Office of the Australian Information Commissioner，隐私监管机构 |
| NDB scheme | Notifiable Data Breaches，数据泄露强制通知制度 |
| TGA | Therapeutic Goods Administration，医疗器械/药品监管 |
| SaMD | Software as a Medical Device |
| ARTG | Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods |
| ACL | Australian Consumer Law |
| ASD Essential Eight | 澳洲信号局的八项基础网络安全缓解措施 |
